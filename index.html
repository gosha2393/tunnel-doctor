Tunnel Doctor

Diagnostics for your own Xray / VLESS servers

Tunnel Doctor shows which of your servers are healthy, slow or down — and why. It checks latency, jitter and loss for every profile, finds configuration mistakes and tests the VPN tunnel end to end.

The app does not provide VPN servers. It works only with servers and subscriptions you add yourself.
